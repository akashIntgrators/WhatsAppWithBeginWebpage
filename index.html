to the next chat.

Open WhatsApp Web from the phone field in Bigin.

The inspected extension adds a chat button and stores a local mapping of the phone, lead name, and record path. It complements the server’s conversation-capture workflow.

Source reviewed · release validation pending. The extension does not send its stored lead mapping to the inspected backend. Installation package is under review.

04 / UNDER THE HOOD

Small parts. A clear flow.

WhatsApp sessions→Node.js + SQLite→Transcript→Bigin note

For teams

Review prior conversation context alongside CRM contacts. Transcripts include direction, timestamps, and participating agents.

For technical reviewers

The baseline uses whatsapp-web.js, persistent local sessions, Express, SQLite, and Bigin contact/note APIs. Production acceptance remains to be independently verified.
